# Brag Plan: AskTube

## What is this app?
AskTube turns a YouTube playlist into a searchable knowledge source: load it once, ask questions about the ideas inside, and get answers grounded in the source videos.

## The angle
Treat AskTube like a quiet research superpower: instead of scrubbing through a playlist, hand it the whole source and ask for the exact idea you need. The visual joke is the shift from “a pile of videos” to one calm, cited answer.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“Your playlist, understood.” lands over the AskTube wordmark and a single playlist URL being loaded.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The playlist moves through “Reading playlist… / Processing videos… / Understanding transcripts…” into a ready state.
- The AskTube question “Where is binary search explained?” appears in the conversation panel.
- A grounded answer resolves with source-video cards and timestamps.

## Outro / punchline
“Stop scrubbing. Start asking.” closes on the AskTube wordmark.

## User flow worth showing
Paste a YouTube playlist URL → AskTube indexes the videos and transcripts → ask a question → receive a grounded answer with source videos.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film for a smarter way to learn
- Interpretation: confident pacing, editorial typography, restrained motion, and crisp UI details rather than loud feature shouting.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f7f8fa
- Accent: #3468f5
- Text: #20242b
- Display font: Georgia / Times New Roman
- Body font: Arial
- Strongest visual element: the split knowledge-source and conversation workspace with blue status accents and green grounded-answer state.

## Share copy (draft)
AskTube turns any YouTube playlist into a knowledge source you can question — grounded answers, linked back to the exact videos that taught it.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, polished rhythmic bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: gentle fade in, a clear lift at the question reveal, soft fade under the final wordmark.
- Music cue guidance: use composition timing as the cue grid; major reveals at 2.5s, 8.5s, and 15.5s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let blue accents carry a restrained presence.
- SFX posture: sparse and motion-matched.
- Audio-coupled moments: URL submit, indexing status ticks, question reveal, source-card arrival.
- Restraint rule: audio must support the feeling of clarity, never overwhelm the copy.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The source — 3.5s
The AskTube wordmark and “Your playlist, understood” appear. A YouTube playlist URL types into the input and the blue Load Playlist button settles.
Sequential/interaction: yes — URL text appears, then the button.
Audio intent: establish a calm, premium pulse.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle key/click accent on the button.
Music: warm upbeat bed.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The work happens — 4.5s
The UI becomes a progress card: “Reading playlist…”, “Processing videos…”, “Understanding transcripts…”, with the progress bar moving from 28% to 100%.
Sequential/interaction: yes — status lines arrive one by one.
Audio intent: give the indexing motion a sense of momentum.
Audio-coupled idea: quiet ticks on progress changes.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Ask the idea — 4.0s
The split workspace is revealed: the playlist is indexed and ready on the left, while “Ask your playlist” anchors the conversation pane. A question bubble reads “Where is binary search explained?”
Sequential/interaction: yes — the question types in, then the ready label appears.
Audio intent: lift at the product payoff.
Audio-coupled idea: beat-aligned question reveal.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Grounded answer — 5.0s
The answer arrives: “Binary search works by cutting the search space in half each time.” Below it, source cards show “Binary Search — The intuition” and “Divide and conquer patterns”, each with a timestamp.
Sequential/interaction: yes — answer first, source cards second.
Audio intent: make citations feel like the proof, not decoration.
Audio-coupled idea: card arrival accents.
Transition mood: polished → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The new default — 3.0s
Large closing line: “Stop scrubbing. Start asking.” AskTube wordmark and “Your playlist, understood.”
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: clean final resolve.
Audio-coupled idea: final logo hit.
Music: fade out.
Transition mood: soft → end
